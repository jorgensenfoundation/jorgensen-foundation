Subscription Terms

By subscribing to the Jorgensen Foundation platform, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. Subscriptions are offered on a monthly or annual basis and grant you access to the platform's computational chemistry programs, cloud computing resources, and associated tools during the active subscription period.

Academic Subscriptions

Academic pricing is available to individuals affiliated with accredited universities, colleges, and non-profit research institutes. Your eligibility is determined automatically by your email domain at the time of registration. If your institution uses a non-standard domain, you must provide verification of your affiliation before academic pricing is applied. Academic subscriptions are priced at $49 per month (monthly plan) or $490 per year (annual plan), equivalent to ten months at the monthly rate.

Industry Subscriptions

Industry pricing applies to all subscribers who do not qualify for academic pricing. Industry subscriptions are priced at $199 per month (monthly plan) or $1,990 per year (annual plan). Industry subscribers receive priority queue access, a dedicated technical support contact, and API access for integration with internal pipelines in addition to all features included in the academic tier.

Subscriptions are billed in advance. Monthly subscriptions are billed on the same day each month. Annual subscriptions are billed once per year on the anniversary of the subscription start date. Payment is processed by Stripe. By subscribing, you authorise the Jorgensen Foundation to charge your payment method on the applicable billing cycle. All prices are in US dollars and exclusive of any applicable taxes, which are your responsibility.

Each subscription is for a single named user. Sharing login credentials or allowing multiple individuals to access the platform under a single subscription is not permitted. Organisations requiring access for multiple users should contact us to discuss multi-seat arrangements.

Cancellation and Refund Policy

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period — you retain full access to the platform until that date.

Monthly Subscriptions

If you cancel a monthly subscription, your access continues until the end of the current monthly billing period. No partial-month refunds are issued. Your saved data, simulation inputs, and results remain accessible for 90 days after cancellation, after which they are permanently deleted.

Annual Subscriptions

If you cancel an annual subscription, your access continues until the end of the current annual term. We do not offer partial-year refunds on annual subscriptions. If you believe your circumstances warrant an exception, please contact us at info@jorgensenfoundation.org and we will consider your request at our discretion.

Refunds

Refunds are not issued for unused subscription periods except where required by applicable consumer protection law. If you experience a technical failure that prevents you from using the platform for a sustained period and you have notified us promptly, we may issue a pro-rated credit at our discretion. We do not issue refunds for dissatisfaction with simulation results, as the accuracy of computational results depends on inputs and parameters outside our control.

We reserve the right to suspend or terminate your account without notice if you violate these Terms of Service, in particular the Acceptable Use provisions in Section 3. In the event of termination for cause, no refund will be issued.

Prohibited Uses
